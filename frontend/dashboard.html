Overview

Revenue leakage types

Healthcare organizations lose significant revenue due to unnoticed errors in billing, coding mistakes, missing charges, and claim submission gaps. These revenue leakages often remain undetected because healthcare data is distributed across multiple systems such as EHR, billing systems, and clearinghouses.

RCM teams spend considerable time manually auditing claims and identifying lost revenue opportunities.

Impact

These issues lead to significant financial loss and require extensive manual auditing by RCM teams.

Detection Engine

Explainable detection rules

Missing charge0 findings

Rule: Encounter has no matching claim, and encounter is more than 3 days old.

Action: Verify charge was entered; submit claim if missing.

Coding error0 findings

Rule: Billed CPT code does not match documented CPT for the same encounter.

Action: Review coding; correct claim or rebill with documented CPT.

Unsubmitted claim0 findings

Rule: Claim exists with status not_submitted, more than 7 days since encounter.

Action: Submit claim to payer or investigate billing hold.

Denied, no follow-up0 findings

Rule: Claim denied more than 45 days ago with no resubmission.

Action: Appeal denial or resubmit corrected claim before deadline.

Underpayment0 findings

Rule: Paid amount is more than 2% below contracted fee schedule rate.

Action: Review remittance advice; file underpayment appeal with payer.
